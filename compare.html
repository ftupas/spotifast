Spotifast: Remove from queue for Next up rows

Baseline: main at 6a60c30. Candidate: branch remove-next-up-from-queue at 550f391. Demo mode (--demo), macOS arm64.

Change under review: a row under Next up in the queue panel now offers Remove from queue in its … / right-click menu (before: only rows under Playing next had it). In the Winamp playlist window, REM → Remove selected is enabled when a Next up row is selected.

The Next up row (Sapien, selected) in the Winamp playlist window with the REM menu open. Before: Remove selected is disabled. After: enabled.

How these were captured

  • Images are the app's own egui render of its window, written by the demo build's --demo-shot flag. They are not macOS window captures: screencapture failed because this process has no Screen Recording permission, and that grant needs a manual GUI step.
  • The right-click and the menu/REM clicks were scripted through the demo build's pointer hook. That hook is throwaway: it was added to src/entrypoint.rs identically in both worktrees (/tmp/sf-before, /tmp/sf-after), was never committed to either PR branch, and reads a SF_CLICKS environment variable with window-local coordinates.
  • Demo flags: --demo --demo-page home --demo-show queue,playing-next,{dark|light} (winamp,playlist,playing-next for the Winamp view). playing-next seeds the Playing next section and starts local playback, which the removal needs.
  • Normal shots are 2560x1600 (2x of 1280x800). Narrow shots are 2000x1440 (2x of 1000x720). The Winamp shot is 550x580 at 1x.
